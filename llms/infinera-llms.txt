# Infinera

> Infinera is a vertically integrated optical networking and DWDM transport
> equipment vendor (coherent optical engines / ICE, XR optics, GX/Groove/FlexILS
> systems, photonic integrated circuits) serving carriers, cloud/ICP, and
> enterprise transport networks. Infinera was acquired by Nokia (completed 2025)
> and now operates as part of Nokia Optical Networks. Programmability is delivered
> through open transport-networking standards (ONF TAPI, OpenConfig, gNMI/gNXI,
> NETCONF/YANG) and infrastructure automation (Terraform, Ansible) rather than a
> single proprietary REST API.
